## Mission Statement

**Personus enables personal agency in the agentic era.**

We are building the personal data control plane for a world where AI agents increasingly act on our behalf — discovering, connecting, transacting. In this world, people need a way to control what gets shared, with whom, and under what terms. Personus gives every person and organization a structured, privacy-preserving digital identity that AI agents can discover, query, and interact with — all under the individual's sovereign control.

---

## Public Benefit Corporation

Personus is structured as a **Public Benefit Corporation (PBC)** — a for-profit entity with a legally binding commitment to social benefit alongside shareholder returns. This is not marketing language; it is governance structure.

### Our Public Benefit Purpose

> To advance personal agency and economic dignity by ensuring that every person controls their own professional identity, reputation, and data — free from platform extraction, surveillance advertising, and algorithmic manipulation.

### What This Means in Practice

**For users:** Your data is yours. Your personas are yours. Your endorsements are yours. We will never sell your data, gate your discoverability behind a paywall, or manipulate your visibility with algorithms designed to extract engagement. These commitments are architectural — enforced by the system's design, not just policy.

**For communities:** Communities on Personus — guilds, teams, chapters, networks — own their membership data and community intelligence. The platform provides infrastructure; the community retains sovereignty. Revenue sharing with community leaders ensures that the people who build and nurture communities participate in the value they create.

**For the network:** Every design decision is evaluated against its impact on the health of the trust network, not just its revenue potential. Features that grow the network through generosity (endorsements, recommendations, welcoming newcomers) are rewarded. Features that would extract value from user-generated content are rejected — even when they would be profitable.

**For society:** By making human capabilities discoverable through trust-backed AI search rather than pay-to-play advertising, Personus reduces friction in the economy. A plumber endorsed by three neighbors is discoverable without paying for leads. A career-changer's hidden skills surface through endorsements, not job titles. Trust flows through the network, creating economic opportunity for people who have been invisible to traditional platforms.

### Alignment with B Corp Principles

| B Corp Dimension | Personus Commitment |

## The Problem We Solve

### The Extraction Economy

The dominant platforms for professional discovery — LinkedIn, Thumbtack, Angi, ZipRecruiter — share a common business model: **users create valuable profiles and content for free, then the platform gates access to that content and charges others to see, search, or contact those users.**

- LinkedIn earns $17.1B/year largely by charging recruiters to search profiles that professionals built for free
- Thumbtack sells each customer inquiry to 3-5 contractors at $15-200 per lead — 80% of whom lose money
- ZipRecruiter charges employers to search resumes that job seekers uploaded at no cost
- Nextdoor harvests hyperlocal user data to sell targeted advertising

Users are the product. Their data, their content, their network — monetized without their meaningful participation in the value created.

### The Agentic Disruption

In the agentic era, this model breaks further. AI agents can route around paywalls, scrape profiles, and disintermediate platforms entirely. The response from incumbents — more aggressive gating, more restrictive free tiers — accelerates user frustration and agent circumvention. It is a death spiral disguised as a business model.

### Personus's Alternative

We believe the sustainable business model for the AI age is one where **the platform creates value that cannot be routed around** — matching intelligence, trust verification, privacy mediation, and community infrastructure — while keeping the network itself open and equitable.

## Three Non-Negotiable Principles

These principles are not aspirational. They are architectural constraints enforced by the system's design.

### 1. Discovery Is Never Gated

Every persona — free or paid — is equally discoverable by AI agents and human search. We do not create a two-class system where paying users rank higher. The free version of Personus is genuinely useful, not a crippled trial designed to frustrate users into upgrading.

### 2. User Data Is Never Sold

Not to advertisers. Not to data brokers. Not to enterprises. Not in aggregate. Revenue comes from services around data (matching, privacy mediation, analytics, infrastructure) — never from the data itself.

### 3. Free Tiers Are Generous

The network grows when people use it. Aggressive gating of the free tier kills network effects. The free tier supports the full core experience for individuals and reasonably-sized groups. We invest in free users because every free user is a node in the trust network that makes the entire platform more valuable.

## Revenue Architecture: Four Tiers

Personus serves four distinct user archetypes, each with a Base and Pro level:

| Tier | Base | Pro | Who It's For |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Solo** | Free | $12/mo | Individuals building personas and discovering people |
| **Community Organizer** | $99/year | $199/mo | Leaders of communities, guilds, membership organizations |
| **Pathfinder** | $49/mo | $149/mo (team) | Recruiters, talent scouts, business development, anyone doing systematic people discovery |
| **Enterprise** | $499/mo | Custom | Organizations wanting internal talent intelligence and external discovery at scale |

**Revenue flows from three sources:**

1. **Services around data** — AI coaching, analytics, skill market intelligence, privacy mediation
2. **Mediation of value exchange** — Fees on structured professional engagements, commerce transactions, guild offerings
3. **Infrastructure access** — MCP/API queries by AI agents, enterprise integrations, community management tools

**Revenue never flows from:** hiding profiles, selling data, charging for discovery, advertising, or penalizing free users in search.

## The Generosity Engine: Sparks

Personus introduces **Sparks** — a credit system that rewards the generous behaviors that grow the trust network. Endorsing someone, recommending a non-user, responding to introduction requests, welcoming newcomers — these acts of generosity are the network's growth engine. Sparks make them visible, recognized, and rewarded.

Sparks are earned through generosity, never purchased with money. They unlock temporary access to premium features, earn visible badges on personas, and create a culture where giving is celebrated rather than extracted.

## Growth Model

The network grows through a flywheel driven by endorsements and community formation:

```
User creates persona → endorses trusted people →
  Shadow personas created for non-users → shadows discoverable by AI →
    Shadows found via search → claim invites sent →
      New users join → endorse THEIR network → flywheel accelerates
```

Communities amplify this flywheel. Every community, guild, and team is a growth engine that brings in members who then extend the network beyond the community's boundaries.

**Critical design decision:** There is no limit on the number of communities any user can create. Every community someone creates is a demand signal and a growth engine. We never want to throttle this impulse.

## Market Position

Personus occupies a unique position at the intersection of three macro trends:

1. **The death of seat-based pricing** — AI agents make per-seat models obsolete; revenue follows work units (queries, introductions, outcomes)
2. **Agentic commerce needs an identity layer** — Stripe/OpenAI ACP, Visa TAP, Mastercard Agent Pay all need privacy-preserving identity mediation
3. **Ecosystem Orchestrators win** — MIT research shows 58% of companies now operate as Ecosystem Drivers, up from 12% in 2013

No incumbent credibly occupies this position. LinkedIn is an extraction platform. Bluesky is pre-revenue with no professional discovery. The community platforms (Circle, Mighty Networks) lack AI-native discovery. The lead-generation platforms (Thumbtack, Angi) are in structural decline.

## Social Impact Thesis

Personus creates measurable social benefit through three mechanisms:

### 1. Economic Inclusion

By making human capabilities discoverable through trust-backed AI search rather than pay-to-play advertising, Personus reduces barriers to economic participation. A skilled tradesperson endorsed by their community is discoverable without paying for leads. A career-changer's transferable skills surface through endorsements, not job titles or credentials.

### 2. Community Sovereignty

Communities on Personus own their membership data and community intelligence. Unlike Facebook Groups (where Meta owns the data) or LinkedIn Groups (where Microsoft controls visibility), Personus communities operate on infrastructure they pay for with data they control. Revenue sharing ensures community leaders are compensated for their labor.

### 3. Privacy as a Right

In a world of AI agents acting on our behalf, privacy is not a luxury — it is a prerequisite for agency. Personus's architecture enforces privacy by design: no PII exposure, mediated contact, GDPR-inspired consent, per-persona visibility controls. Users participate in the agentic economy without surrendering control of their personal data.
