# Personus.ai — The Generosity Engine: Sparks

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 2026-02-17
**Audience:** Stakeholders, investors, advisors
**Status:** Draft for review

---

## Why Generosity Needs a System

Every social platform depends on user contributions — content, connections, recommendations. The dominant platforms treat these contributions as free labor to be extracted. LinkedIn endorsements generate no tangible value for the endorser. Yelp reviews enrich the platform while the reviewer gets nothing. Nextdoor neighbor recommendations build a data asset that Nextdoor monetizes through advertising.

Personus takes the opposite approach. **The generous behaviors that grow the trust network — endorsing, recommending, welcoming, responding — are recognized, rewarded, and celebrated.** This is not altruism; it is architecture. A network that rewards generosity grows faster, retains better, and creates a culture that attracts the kinds of people who make the network valuable.

Sparks is the system that makes generosity visible and tangible.

---

## What Are Sparks?

Sparks are a credit system earned exclusively through acts of generosity that strengthen the trust network. They are:

- **Earned**, never purchased with money
- **Visible** on your persona as badges and status
- **Redeemable** for temporary access to premium features
- **Multiplied** by paid tiers (rewarding investment in the platform)
- **Social proof** that you are a generous, active member of the network

Sparks are not a cryptocurrency, a tradeable token, or a financial instrument. They are a gamification mechanic designed to make the generous behaviors that grow the network feel rewarding and fun.

## How Sparks Are Earned

### Core Actions

| Action | Sparks | Why It Matters to the Network |
|---|---|---|
| **Endorse someone** | 5 | Endorsements are the core trust signal. Each one strengthens the network's value for everyone. |
| **Write a detailed endorsement** (50+ words, specific skills referenced) | 10 | Higher-quality endorsements improve AI search relevance and trust chain strength. |
| **Create a shadow persona** (recommend a non-user) | 8 | Directly grows the network. Creates a discoverable entity and sends a claim invitation. |
| **Respond to an introduction request** (accept or decline) | 5 | Keeps the network responsive. Unreturned requests erode trust in the system. |
| **Accept an introduction request** | 3 | Completing the connection loop. |
| **Invite someone to Personus** (who signs up) | 10 | Direct user acquisition through word of mouth — the highest-trust growth channel. |
| **Invite someone to a community** (who joins) | 5 | Grows community density, which improves group-scoped discovery. |
| **Welcome a shadow persona claim** (the person you recommended joins and claims their persona) | 15 | Full growth loop: you recommended them → they joined → the network is richer. This is the highest-value action. |

### Persona & Profile Actions

| Action | Sparks | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Complete your persona to 80%+** | 20 (one-time) | Richer personas improve search quality for everyone. |
| **Add a new trait category** (e.g., first time adding offerings) | 5 (one-time per category) | Deeper profiles create better matches. |
| **Update your persona** (quarterly refresh) | 3 | Keeps the network's data fresh and relevant. |

### Community Actions

| Action | Sparks | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Be the first to respond to a guild request** | 5 | Rewards responsiveness in guilds. Fast response is a key quality signal. |
| **Complete a guild request fulfillment** | 10 | Successful guild interactions prove the model works. |
| **Steward actions** (review applications, route requests) | 3 per action | Rewards community leadership labor — the unpaid work that makes communities function. |
| **Welcome a new community member** (first interaction) | 2 | Creates a warm onboarding experience. |

### Re-engagement

| Action | Sparks | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Return after 7+ days away** | 2 | Gently rewards re-engagement without creating anxiety about streaks. |

### What Does NOT Earn Sparks

- Logging in (no "daily check-in" mechanic — we don't want empty engagement)
- Searching (discovery is a right, not a rewardable action)
- Viewing profiles (consumption is passive; creation and generosity are active)
- Purchasing a paid tier (money and Sparks are separate economies)
- Self-endorsement or reciprocal-only endorsement patterns (anti-gaming)

## How Sparks Are Spent

Sparks unlock temporary access to premium features, creating natural "taste" moments that drive tier upgrades. They also unlock permanent badges that serve as social proof.

### Temporary Feature Unlocks

| Redemption | Cost | Duration | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **+5 introduction requests** | 20 Sparks | This month | Extends your monthly intro limit |
| **+5 Coach sessions** | 25 Sparks | This month | Extends your monthly Coach limit |
| **Trust chain +1 hop** | 15 Sparks | 7 days | See one level deeper in the trust graph |
| **Skill demand insight** (one skill) | 10 Sparks | One-time | See demand trends for a specific skill |
| **Community boost on Explore** | 30 Sparks | 7 days | Your community gets featured placement |
| **Early access to new features** | 75 Sparks | Until general release | Beta access to upcoming capabilities |

### Permanent Badges

Badges appear on your persona and serve as visible social proof. They are auto-awarded when cumulative Spark thresholds are reached in specific categories.

| Badge | Threshold | Displayed As |
|---|---|---|
| **Generous Connector** | 50 Sparks from endorsement actions | Visible on persona profile — signals you actively endorse others |
| **Community Builder** | 100 Sparks from community-related actions | Signals you build and nurture communities |
| **Trusted Voice** | 100 Sparks from endorsement actions | Signals your endorsements carry weight (volume + quality) |
| **Welcome Wagon** | 50 Sparks from invitation/welcome actions | Signals you bring new people into the network |
| **Guild Champion** | 100 Sparks from guild-related actions | Signals active, responsive guild participation |

Badges are not vanity metrics. They are **trust signals that AI agents can factor into search results.** When an AI is deciding between two equally-skilled people, the one with a "Trusted Voice" badge — earned through dozens of thoughtful endorsements — is a stronger recommendation.

## Spark Multipliers by Tier

Paid users earn Sparks faster, reflecting the principle that invested users who are also generous are the most valuable members of the network.

| Tier | Spark Earn Multiplier | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Solo Free | 1x | Baseline — free users are still rewarded for generosity |
| Solo Pro | 1.5x | Pro users tend to be more active; multiplier rewards this |
| Community Organizer (Base or Pro) | 2x | Community leaders drive the most network growth per person |
| Pathfinder (Base or Team) | 1.5x | Pathfinders who endorse and introduce grow the network |
| Enterprise (Base or Pro) | 2x | Enterprise users bring entire organizations into the network |

**Example:** A Community Organizer who endorses someone earns 10 Sparks (5 base × 2x multiplier) instead of 5.

## Anti-Gaming Measures

Any reward system attracts gaming. Sparks are designed with built-in resistance:

### Diminishing Returns

- Endorsing the **same person** multiple times earns Sparks only on the first endorsement
- Endorsing more than **10 people in a single day** triggers a cooldown (no additional Sparks until the next day) — this prevents mass-endorsement spam
- Creating more than **5 shadow personas in a single day** triggers a cooldown — prevents fake-shadow farming

### Quality Signals

- Detailed endorsements (50+ words, specific skills) earn **2x** the Sparks of brief endorsements — rewarding quality over quantity
- Endorsements that are later referenced in search results (i.e., the endorsed person gets found because of your endorsement) earn **bonus Sparks** retroactively — rewarding endorsements that create real value

### Pattern Detection

- Reciprocal-only endorsement patterns (A endorses B, B endorses A, nothing else) are flagged and earn reduced Sparks
- Newly created accounts that immediately endorse dozens of people before building their own persona earn no Sparks until their persona reaches 50% completeness
- Bulk invitation patterns that generate bounced emails or non-signups reduce future invitation Sparks

### The Fundamental Protection

The most important anti-gaming measure is that **Sparks cannot be purchased or converted to money.** There is no financial incentive to game the system. The rewards are premium feature access and social badges — valuable to genuine users, worthless to bots.

## Social Benefit Alignment

The Sparks system directly advances Personus's public benefit purpose:

### Economic Inclusion

Sparks reward the act of making others visible. When you endorse a skilled tradesperson, a career-changer, or a newcomer to your field, you are directly expanding their economic opportunity. Sparks make this labor visible and valued.

### Community Sovereignty

Community-related Sparks (steward actions, welcoming members, responding to guild requests) recognize the unpaid labor of community leadership. This is a step toward the broader goal of community leaders participating in the economics of the communities they build.

### Trust Network Health

The Spark earn/spend design is calibrated to reward behaviors that make the trust network healthier — more endorsements, higher quality endorsements, faster response times, more welcoming onboarding. These are public goods that benefit everyone on the network, not just the individual earning Sparks.

## Spark Economy Calibration

### Expected Earn Velocity

For a moderately generous user on the free tier (1x multiplier):

| Activity Level | Weekly Actions | Weekly Sparks | Monthly Sparks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Casual** | 1 endorsement + 1 intro response | 10 | ~40 |
| **Active** | 3 endorsements + 1 shadow + 1 invite | 33 | ~130 |
| **Power user** | 5 endorsements + 2 shadows + guild steward work | 50+ | ~200+ |

A casual free user earning ~40 Sparks/month can redeem one meaningful bonus per month (5 extra intros for 20 Sparks, or a trust chain expansion for 15 Sparks). This is enough to experience premium value and consider upgrading.

A power user on Solo Pro (1.5x multiplier) earning ~200+ Sparks/month accumulates badges quickly and has a steady stream of feature unlocks. They are deeply engaged and highly unlikely to churn.

### Spend Velocity

The goal is for most active users to **spend Sparks at roughly the rate they earn them** — not to accumulate massive hoards. This keeps the economy flowing and ensures feature unlocks feel like regular rewards, not rare events.

If we observe hoarding (users accumulating 500+ Sparks without spending), we may introduce higher-value redemption options or charitable donations (e.g., "Donate 100 Sparks to give a free year of Solo Pro to a community leader in an underserved area").
