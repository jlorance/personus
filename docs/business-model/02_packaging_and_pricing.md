## Pricing Philosophy

Personus pricing is built on three convictions:

1. **Charge for services, not for access to people.** We never gate discovery, hide profiles, or sell leads. We charge for matching intelligence, community infrastructure, privacy mediation, and AI-powered tools.

2. **Every tier has a Base and a Pro.** This creates a natural upgrade path within each user archetype. No one is forced to jump from free to enterprise. The Base tier at each level is priced to be an easy decision; the Pro tier delivers premium value for power users.

3. **Free is generous, not crippled.** The free tier supports the full core experience. Endorsements are always unlimited. Discovery ranking is always equal. Limits exist to manage infrastructure costs and prevent abuse, not to frustrate users into upgrading.

The Community Organizer tier serves anyone who leads a group on Personus — neighborhood communities, professional guilds, volunteer teams, membership chapters, interest networks. It attaches to **individual communities**, not to the user. A person can create unlimited communities; each community starts free (up to 200 members) and upgrades independently when it outgrows the free tier.

### How Community Pricing Works

Every community starts with the free capabilities that come with any user's tier (Solo Free or Solo Pro):
- Up to 200 members

- Basic member directory and group-scoped search

When a community outgrows 200 members or needs advanced features, the community's administrator(s) upgrade that specific community to Community Organizer Base or Pro.

### Why CO Base at $99/year is an Easy Decision

$99/year = $8.25/month. For context:
- A Slack Pro workspace for 200 users costs $1,750/mo
- A Circle.so Professional plan costs $89/mo for basic community hosting
- A Meetup Pro organizer subscription costs $199/year

At $99/year, a community organizer gets: 1,000 members, guild features, analytics, custom branding, and the most advanced AI-powered member discovery of any community platform. This is priced to remove friction, not to maximize per-customer revenue. The value to Personus is the 1,000 members each upgraded community brings into the network.

The aggregate capability report alone is worth $199/mo for any serious organization. Consider:

For membership organizations (The Nature Conservancy, Rotary, professional associations), the ability to answer "What can our members collectively do?" and "Who can we mobilize for [specific initiative]?" is strategic intelligence that has never been available at this price point.

## Pricing Principles: What We Will Never Do

As a Public Benefit Corporation, we commit to the following pricing principles:

1. **We will never charge for basic discovery.** Free users will always be discoverable by AI agents at equal ranking to paid users.

2. **We will never sell user data.** Not to advertisers, data brokers, or enterprises. Not in aggregate, anonymized, or pseudonymized form.

3. **We will never use opaque algorithmic pricing.** Every price is published. No dynamic pricing based on user behavior, urgency, or willingness to pay.

4. **We will never lock users into annual contracts with termination penalties.** Users can downgrade or cancel at any time. The $99/year Community Organizer price is an annual commitment by choice, not by trap.

5. **We will never degrade the free tier to force upgrades.** If anything, the free tier will become more generous over time as the network grows and our unit economics improve.

6. **We will share revenue with community leaders.** Guild stewards and community organizers who drive value through their communities participate in the economics, not just the labor.
